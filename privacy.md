# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

This privacy policy applies to the **Air Fryer Converter** app (the
"App") on both iOS and Android. It is published on the Apple App Store
and on Google Play as "Oven to Air Fryer Converter", under the
identifier `com.brynneprince.airfryerconverter`.

The App is a recipe-conversion utility. We try to collect the absolute
minimum data needed for the App to function, and to be transparent about
the third-party services that do collect data on our behalf.

## Who is responsible

The App is published by **Brynne Prince**, an individual developer based
in South Africa. Brynne Prince decides how and why the data described
here is used (the "controller" under European law, the "responsible
party" under South African law). Contact details are at the end of this
page.

## Data stored on your device

The App stores your saved recipes, unit preferences, theme choice, and
analytics preference locally on your device using the operating system's
standard storage. Recipe names, notes, temperatures, and other recipe
content are not included in product analytics.

Cooking timer alerts are scheduled by your phone's own notification
system. They are made and shown on the device, and nothing about them
is sent to us or to any third party. You can turn them off in your
phone's notification settings.

## Data collected by third-party services

The App uses third-party services for anonymous product analytics,
advertising, processing the optional in-app purchase that removes ads,
and delivering app updates.

### PostHog (product analytics)

When anonymous analytics is enabled, the App sends a limited set of
events to PostHog's EU-hosted service. These events include:

- App opens and app/device version information.
- Use of conversion, timer, and saved-recipe features.
- Counts such as the number of saved recipes or active timers.
- Coarse timer-duration ranges rather than exact cooking values.
- Ad impressions and revenue, paywall views, and purchase outcomes.
- Device model, operating system, language and screen size, which
  PostHog's SDK adds to each event.

PostHog also receives your IP address with each request, as any web
service does. Looking up your location from it is turned off.

PostHog assigns a random identifier to an installation so that we can
measure return usage. We do not send your name, email address, recipe
names, recipe notes, exact temperatures, or advertising identifier to
PostHog. Session replay and automatic touch or text capture are disabled.

You can stop future product analytics at any time in the App's Settings
screen by turning off **Share anonymous analytics**.

PostHog's privacy policy:
https://posthog.com/privacy

### Google AdMob (advertising)

The App displays banner ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

- **Device identifiers** — used to deliver personalized ads. On iOS
  this is the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only when you allow it
  in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. On Android this is the
  advertising ID, unless you have deleted or reset it in Android
  settings.
- **Usage data** (which screens of the App you've opened, ad
  interactions) — used for ad measurement and analytics.
- **Approximate location**, derived from your IP address — used to
  choose relevant ads and to meet regional consent rules.
- **Diagnostics** (crash and performance data from the ads component).

If you decline tracking on iOS, delete your advertising ID on Android,
or decline personalized ads in the consent message shown where the law
requires one, AdMob shows **non-personalized** ads, chosen from things
like the screen they appear on and your approximate location. If you
decline all choices in that consent message, the App may show no ads
at all.

Google's full privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

### RevenueCat (in-app purchase processing)

Where the in-app purchase is offered, buying **Air Fryer Pro** (the
one-time purchase that removes ads, from **Get Pro** in Settings) uses
RevenueCat to validate the purchase receipt with Apple's or Google's
servers and to remember your purchase across reinstalls. RevenueCat may
collect:

- **An anonymous user identifier** generated automatically by their
  SDK on first launch.
- **Purchase history** for the App (which products you've bought,
  when, and the price).
- **The store receipt** for the purchase, which it checks with Apple
  or Google.
- **Your IP address**, which it receives with each request.
- **Device platform and OS version** for analytics.

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, or any
information you typed into the App.

RevenueCat's full privacy policy:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### Expo (app updates)

When the App starts, it asks Expo's update service whether a newer
version of the App's code is available, and downloads it if so. To
answer, Expo receives your IP address, your device platform and OS
version, and the App's version and update ID. Expo does not receive
your recipes, settings or any information you typed into the App.

Expo's privacy policy:
https://expo.dev/privacy

### Apple and Google payments

When you make an in-app purchase, Apple (on iOS) or Google Play (on
Android) processes the payment through your store account. We never see
your payment details. The store's own privacy policy applies to that
flow:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Why this data is used

Where the law asks for a legal reason, these are ours:

- **Personalized ads** — where the law requires consent (such as in the
  EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and on iOS everywhere), your consent,
  given in the consent message or the tracking prompt. You can withdraw
  it at any time. Elsewhere, our legitimate interest in funding a free
  App; you can opt out at any time as described below.
- **Non-personalized ads and ad measurement** — our legitimate interest
  in funding a free App.
- **Product analytics** — our legitimate interest in understanding which
  features are used. You can switch it off in Settings.
- **The Pro purchase** — to carry out the purchase you asked for
  and to restore it later.
- **App updates** — our legitimate interest in keeping the App working
  and fixing faults quickly.

## How long data is kept

We do not keep our own copy of any of this data. Data on your device
stays there until you delete it or remove the App. Data held by PostHog,
Google, RevenueCat and Expo is kept only for as long as each service needs it
for the purposes above, under that service's own retention rules, which
are described in the policies linked above.

## Where data is processed

PostHog stores analytics on servers in the European Union. Google,
RevenueCat and Expo may process data in the United States and other countries.
Where data leaves the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or
South Africa, those services rely on the safeguards described in their
own policies, such as standard contractual clauses.

## Selling and sharing (United States)

We do not sell your personal information for money. Showing personalized
ads through Google AdMob may count as "selling" or "sharing" under the
laws of some US states. You can opt out at any time through **Ad privacy
options** in the App's Settings (shown where it applies), by declining
tracking on iOS, or by deleting your advertising ID on Android. We do
not treat you differently for opting out.

## Advertising identifier and tracking

**iOS.** The App asks permission to use your device's advertising
identifier, in line with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.
You may decline at any time, including by changing the setting in iOS
**Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

**Android.** The App uses the Android advertising ID for ads. You can
reset or delete it at any time in Android **Settings → Privacy → Ads**
(the exact path varies by device).

On either platform, declining does not disable any feature of the App;
it only stops AdMob from personalizing the ads you see.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, or under the age at
which your country lets a child agree to data use on their own where
that is higher (up to 16 in parts of Europe). We do not knowingly
collect any data from children. If you believe a child has provided
data to us, please contact us at the address below and we will assist.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- Disable future anonymous product analytics in the App's Settings.
- Request information about or deletion of data held by the third-party
  services listed above. Contact us and we will assist where possible.
  Because product analytics is not linked to your name or email address,
  we may not be able to identify a particular anonymous installation
  from your contact details alone.
- Opt out of personalized advertising at any time, via **Ad privacy
  options** in the App's Settings (shown where it applies), the tracking
  setting in iOS, or the advertising ID setting in Android.
- Ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, and
  object to or withdraw consent for any of the uses above.
- Complain to the data protection authority where you live, for example
  your national authority in the EU, the Information Commissioner's
  Office in the UK, the National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) in
  Brazil, or the Information Regulator in South Africa.
